# Public-content review procedure

Review the complete tracked payload before publication, including scripts, fixtures, generated evidence and historical research.
Keep site instructions, real inventory, raw datasets and runtime/model policy in ignored `local/` or the private mail spool.
Generic public examples use invented identities, reserved example domains and synthetic tasks.
Original site material removed during cleanup is preserved under `local/site-originals/`; the local preservation manifest records hashes.

Check both known identifiers and semantic content such as product decisions, dated incidents and copied dispatches.
If a possible credential appears, stop and report only its location to the authorized owner without quoting its value.
Verify ignored overlays with `git check-ignore` and validate tooling against isolated fixtures.
A current-tree scrub does not remove previously published Git objects.
Send history exposure locations privately to the repository owner; only the owner decides on history remediation.
Do not rewrite history or publish a cleanup without explicit authority.
