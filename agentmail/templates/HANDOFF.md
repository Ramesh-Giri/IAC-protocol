# Summary

What changed or was learned, in a few sentences.

## Requested action

Assess / clarify requirements / review a proposal / implement an approved task.
State which one. Research alone does not authorize implementation.

## Evidence

Pinned repository commits and paths, or the complete small document below.
Separate observed facts from recommendations and assumptions.

## Constraints and authority

Project, affected product/technical domain, existing decision/delegation,
compatibility constraints, and anything explicitly out of scope.

## Useful response

What answer, decision, or verification would let the next person proceed?
Reply with the incoming message ID. Say explicitly if an artifact is missing.
